Privacy & Policy

Learn how SMARTPOINT handles enquiries and information on this website.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

SMARTPOINT respects your privacy. This website is a product showcase with phone, email and WhatsApp enquiry links. Please read this policy to understand how your information may be used.

1. Information You Share

When you contact us, you may voluntarily provide details such as your name, phone number, email address, product of interest and the message you send. This site does not have account registration or an online payment gateway.

2. How We Use Information

Information you provide may be used to reply to enquiries, share product information, provide customer support and respond to your requests.

3. WhatsApp and Email Enquiries

When you click a WhatsApp or email link, you are transferred to the relevant third-party service or email app, whose own privacy practices apply. The contact form launches your email application rather than storing messages on this website.

4. Local Browser Storage

Your enquiry basket is saved locally in your browser using localStorage to make product selection easier. It is not sent to SMARTPOINT until you choose to share an enquiry through WhatsApp. You can clear browser data to remove this saved selection.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may load fonts from Google Fonts and can be hosted through Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. These services may process technical data such as IP addresses under their own policies.

6. Security and Data Retention

We aim to use customer information only for the purpose for which it was shared. Messages sent via external services may be stored by those providers. Please do not send sensitive financial or identity documents through the enquiry form.

7. Demo Listings and Pricing

Listed items, prices, ratings, offers and discounts are illustrative demo content and can change. Contact SMARTPOINT for verified prices, availability, warranties and product specifications before placing an order.

8. Contact for Privacy Questions

For privacy-related requests, call +91 9510013940 or email Dmfaizan@44gmail.com.
